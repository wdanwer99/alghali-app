### Stack and environment
- **D-001 · 2026-10-10 · Owner** — **Hosted Supabase replaces NestJS + Prisma + Redis/BullMQ + Meilisearch + MinIO + docker-compose.** Business rules live in Postgres RPCs/triggers; Edge Functions (TypeScript) handle the slot engine, xlsx import and messaging. *There's no Docker on the dev machine and the owner chose hosted Supabase.*
- **D-002 · 2026-10-10 · Owner** — **Two Flutter apps** (`apps/buyer`, `apps/partner`) plus **Next.js admin** (`apps/admin`), sharing `packages/core`.
- **D-003 · 2026-10-10 · Owner** — Flutter switched globally from the `master` pre-release to the **stable** channel.
- **D-004 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Environments:** hosted `alghali-dev` (apps) and `alghali-test` (automated tests, wiped per run); CI runs `supabase start` on GitHub runners. *Without Docker locally this is the only way to get an isolated test DB.*
- **D-005 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **API contract:** `supabase gen types` → `packages/db-types` (TS). Dart models are hand-declared with `freezed` + `json_serializable` in `packages/core`, with typed RPC wrappers. **Dio dropped**, since `supabase_flutter` covers HTTP. *There's no REST controller layer to generate OpenAPI from.*
- **D-006 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Search** uses Postgres FTS (`simple` config) + `pg_trgm` over a denormalised `listing_search_docs` table maintained by triggers. Normaliser `ar_normalize()`: أ/إ/آ/ٱ→ا, ة→ه, ى→ي, strips tashkeel (U+064B–U+0652, U+0670) and tatweel (U+0640), lower-cases Latin, collapses whitespace. Synonym groups are expanded at query time. *Meilisearch can be put behind the same `search_listings` RPC later if needed.*
- **D-007 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Jobs:** pgmq queue + pg_cron (+ `pg_net` to invoke Edge Functions) replace BullMQ.
- **D-008 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Images:** the client produces `thumb` 160px, `medium` 480px, `large` 1080px (WebP) and an `orig` JPEG capped at 2048px, then uploads all four under one base key. Buckets enforce MIME types and size. *Server-side transforms need a paid Supabase plan; doing it on the client also saves upload data.*

### Money and payments
- **D-010 · 2026-10-10 · Owner** — **Labour price** is set by each technician per job type (`technician_job_types.labour_sdg`), with optional admin min/max in `labour_price_bounds`. "Any available technician" shows a price range, and the assigned technician's price is locked when the booking is created.
- **D-011 · 2026-10-10 · Owner** — **Cash to technician:** the technician collects all cash and hands the part money to the shop at pickup. Both confirm in the app (`confirm_cash_handover`). The shop's commission is netted from its next payout, and the booking fee from the technician's.
- **D-012 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Who earns what:**
  - The booking fee goes to the platform.
  - Labour and travel fee go 100% to the technician.
  - The delivery fee goes to the shop, which does its own delivery.
  - Commission is charged on part sales only, not on delivery, labour or travel.
- **D-013 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Ledger model and worked examples** (acceptance journey; numbers are illustrative): bulbs 12,000, labour 6,000, travel 3,000 (≤3 km band), booking fee 2,000, commission 5% = 600.

  *Bankak (everything paid by transfer, 23,000):*
  | Journal | Dr | Cr | Amount |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | DEPOSIT/payment confirmed | PLATFORM_BANKAK | BUYER_FUNDS_HELD | 23,000 |
  | SALE (sub-order completed) | BUYER_FUNDS_HELD | SHOP:x | 12,000 |
  | COMMISSION | SHOP:x | REVENUE_COMMISSION | 600 |
  | LABOUR + TRAVEL_FEE (booking completed) | BUYER_FUNDS_HELD | TECH:y | 9,000 |
  | BOOKING_FEE | BUYER_FUNDS_HELD | REVENUE_BOOKING_FEE | 2,000 |

  Result: bank 23,000 = shop payable 11,400 + tech payable 9,000 + revenue 2,600 ✔

  *Cash to technician (Bankak deposit 5,000; cash 18,000):*
  | Journal | Dr | Cr | Amount |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | DEPOSIT confirmed | PLATFORM_BANKAK | BUYER_FUNDS_HELD | 5,000 |
  | CASH_COLLECTED by tech | TECH:y | BUYER_FUNDS_HELD | 18,000 |
  | CASH_HANDOVER tech → shop | SHOP:x | TECH:y | 12,000 |
  | SALE | BUYER_FUNDS_HELD | SHOP:x | 12,000 |
  | COMMISSION | SHOP:x | REVENUE_COMMISSION | 600 |
  | LABOUR + TRAVEL_FEE | BUYER_FUNDS_HELD | TECH:y | 9,000 |
  | BOOKING_FEE | BUYER_FUNDS_HELD | REVENUE_BOOKING_FEE | 2,000 |

  Result:
  - The shop owes 600, netted from its next payout.
  - The platform owes the technician 3,000.
  - Revenue is 2,600.
  - The bank holds 5,000 = 3,000 + 2,600 − 600 ✔
  - `BUYER_FUNDS_HELD` ends at 0 ✔
- **D-014 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Rounding:** `commission_sdg = round_half_up(items_sdg × bp / 10000)`, calculated per sub-order. All amounts are whole SDG.
- **D-015 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Price lock at checkout**, not "once paid". `order_items` are immutable after checkout, stock is reserved (`reserved_qty`), and unpaid Bankak orders expire after `payment_window_minutes` (120), releasing stock and holds. `cart_validate` / `checkout` return any price or stock changes for the buyer to accept.
- **D-016 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Order reference** is 8 digits including a Luhn check digit (e.g. `4815 1627`). *Easier and less error-prone to type into a Bankak note than letters.*
- **D-017 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Duplicate receipts:** a partial unique index on `payments.bankak_txn_no` for submitted/confirmed payments blocks reusing a transaction number. Only an admin can move a payment to `CONFIRMED`; a screenshot alone never does.
- **D-018 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Negative balances** (e.g. a cash-only shop) carry forward and are netted from future payouts. The admin sees a "balance owed" report and is alerted when an amount is unpaid for 2 payout cycles. There's no automatic suspension in the MVP.
- **D-019 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Deposit and cancellation:**
  - Cancelling before `cancel_cutoff_hours` gets a full refund.
  - After the cutoff, the deposit is kept: the booking-fee part goes to the platform and the remainder to the technician as travel compensation.
  - If the technician cancels, the job is reassigned or the buyer gets a full refund.
  - "Part does not fit" at check-in: the buyer pays the travel fee only (taken from the deposit, with any rest refunded) and a return opens on the linked sub-order.

### Orders and bookings
- **D-020 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Shop acknowledgement** is a timestamp (`shop_ack_at`), not a state, so the brief's SubOrder enum is unchanged. The admin is alerted if a confirmed sub-order isn't acknowledged within 30 minutes during opening hours.
- **D-021 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Completion:** a `TO_TECHNICIAN` sub-order completes when its booking completes. `PICKED_UP`/`DELIVERED` sub-orders auto-complete after `auto_complete_days` unless a return is requested.
- **D-022 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Slot holds** are `HELD` booking rows with `hold_expires_at`, covered by the `EXCLUDE` constraint. The cron sweeper expires them, and `booking-hold` also expires stale holds for the technician before inserting.
- **D-023 · 2026-10-10 · Owner (Q2 default)** — **The hold becomes `AWAITING_PAYMENT`** on receipt submission and keeps blocking the slot. The technician is offered the job **after the admin confirms payment** (immediately for cash).
- **D-024 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Decline/timeout are offer statuses** (`booking_offers`). The booking stays `PENDING_TECH_ACCEPT` while the next match for the **same time window** is offered. If nobody is free it becomes `NEEDS_RESCHEDULE`. The buyer is notified at each step.
- **D-025 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Travel buffer:** the exclusion range is `block_from..block_to` = the job ± `travel_buffer_minutes` (30) for mobile jobs and the bare job for bay jobs. That way the DB constraint itself enforces travel time.
- **D-026 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Distance** is straight-line (`ST_Distance` on geography) from the street origin for fee bands and from the technician's home point for radius. Road distance can come later.
- **D-027 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Earliest slot** = `now + part_ready_buffer_minutes`, rounded up to 15 minutes, and shifted to the next admin working period for unconfirmed Bankak orders. The grid is 30 minutes; duration is the sum of part → category standard minutes.
- **D-028 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Job code:** 4 random digits, visible only to the buyer (RLS). The technician submits it via `tech_complete`. Five wrong attempts lock completion and alert the admin.
- **D-029 · 2026-10-10 · Default** — **Technician location** is stored in `tech_locations` only while `EN_ROUTE`, is readable only by that booking's buyer, and is deleted at check-in.

### M2 commerce (2026-10-14)
- **D-081 · Default** — **The ledger is double entry and append-only, posted per sub-order.** Every journal's lines sum to zero, checked when the transaction commits. Updates and deletes are refused (`APPEND_ONLY`). With A = items + delivery fee and K = commission (D-013):
  - Bankak paid: `PAYMENT` Dr platform Bankak / Cr buyer funds held (the whole order).
  - Cash taken by the shop: `CASH_COLLECTED` Dr shop / Cr buyer funds held, A.
  - Sub-order completed: `SALE` Dr buyer funds held / Cr shop, A; then `COMMISSION` Dr shop / Cr commission revenue, K.
  - Refund owed: `REFUND` Dr buyer funds held / Cr refunds payable, A. Cash handed back: `CASH_RETURNED`, the reverse of `CASH_COLLECTED`.
  - Returned after the sale: `SALE_REVERSAL` and `COMMISSION_REVERSAL` first, then the refund.
  - Refund sent: `REFUND_PAID`. Payout sent: `PAYOUT` Dr shop / Cr platform Bankak.

  Whatever path an order takes, "buyer funds held" ends at zero for it. The ledger tests check this for Bankak and cash sales, a shop declining after payment, and returns before and after completion. A shop account's credit balance is what the platform owes the shop; a debit balance is what the shop owes (commission on cash it kept). *The shop only earns the money when the sale is final, so cancellations before completion never need a reversal.*
- **D-082 · Default** — **Status changes follow transition tables.** `suborder_transitions` and `payment_transitions` list every allowed (from, to, actor) move, with actor BUYER, SHOP, ADMIN or SYSTEM. A trigger refuses any other change, and any change made without a declared actor, even from the SQL editor. Only `transition_suborder()` and `transition_payment()` declare one, so every change writes its event row and applies its side effects (stock, cash, ledger, refunds, notifications) in one place. *The state machines can be read and tested as tables, and no code path can skip the bookkeeping.*
- **D-083 · Default** — **Cash is collected when the part reaches the buyer.** Pickup: when the shop taps "handed over". Delivery: when it taps "delivered". The cash payment then moves `DUE → COLLECTED`, and the shop owes the commission, which is netted from later payouts (D-018). A cash order no shop has accepted yet can be cancelled by the buyer.
- **D-084 · Default** — **The buyer sends the Bankak transaction number, a receipt screenshot, or both.** At least one is required. The admin must type the number from the bank statement when confirming, if the buyer didn't send it. A unique index means one transfer can confirm only one payment (D-017). A rejected receipt can be sent again until `pay_by`.
- **D-085 · Default** — **A return covers the whole sub-order and is refunded in full**, delivery fee included. The buyer asks within `return_window_days` of receiving the part (also after the order completed), giving a reason, and an admin approves or declines. Approval puts the stock back and reverses the sale. *Partial returns would need per-item refunds and restocking; with one or two parts per shop they aren't worth it at launch.*
- **D-086 · Default** — **Shops see an order only once it is theirs to prepare.** A Bankak order waiting for payment or under review is hidden from the shop. A shop never sees another shop's part of the same order, nor the order's totals. It reads its orders through `shop_orders`, which adds the buyer's name and phone to call.
- **D-087 · Default** — **The delivery fee is per shop**, by straight-line distance (D-026) from the shop's map pin to the buyer's address, using the delivery fee bands. If the shop has no pin, the distance is measured from the street's starting point (D-077). If neither is set, delivery is unavailable (`DELIVERY_UNAVAILABLE`); beyond the last band it is `DELIVERY_OUT_OF_RANGE`.
- **D-088 · Default** — **The platform sends refunds by Bankak; an admin records each one.** A refund is created automatically when a paid sub-order is cancelled, declined by the shop, or returned. Admins see it under Payments → Refunds with the buyer's refund account (`set_refund_account`) and mark it sent with the transaction number. For a cash order, money not yet collected is simply cancelled; collected cash is handed back by the shop (`CASH_RETURNED`).
- **D-089 · Default** — **Payouts are flat amounts from ledger balances**, not lists of orders. "Prepare payouts" creates a draft for every shop the platform owes, minus any payout still waiting, then the admin approves, sends by Bankak and marks it paid with the transaction number. Shops that owe the platform get no payout; each run records them, so the Cash page shows how many runs in a row each has owed (D-018).
- **D-090 · Default** — **Sales complete on their own.** A picked-up or delivered sub-order completes when the buyer taps "I got it", or after `auto_complete_days` (hourly job). Unpaid Bankak orders expire after `pay_by` (job every 5 minutes), except receipts still under review, which wait for the admin. Photo requests close after 3 days.
- **D-091 · Default** — **Alerts are rows in an in-app inbox (`notifications`): a code plus data, never text.** The apps word each alert in the reader's language. M3's dispatcher sends the same rows as push notifications (D-061).
- **D-092 · Default** — **The cart lives on the server; checkout re-checks it.** Signed-in buyers write `cart_items` directly. Guests keep the cart on the phone and check it with `cart_validate`. Each line keeps the price the buyer saw; if any price or stock changed, checkout places nothing and returns `CART_CHANGED` with every line, so the app can show what changed. Shops' offers on photo requests go through the same cart (one each).
- **D-093 · Default** — **Chat is one conversation per buyer, shop and (optionally) sub-order.** Messages are written directly under RLS and delivered by Supabase Realtime. Voice notes (≤ 60 s, D-033) are uploaded first to the private `voice-notes` bucket under the conversation's folder.
- **D-094 · Default** — **The admin Payments, Orders, Cash and Payouts pages now read the real database** (follows D-079). Payments has a Refunds tab. Orders open a detail page where an admin can cancel a shop's part, mark it completed, or decide a return. The sample-data banners are gone from these pages; Bookings, Disputes and technician applications keep theirs until M3.
- **D-095 · Default** — **Demo orders on dev.** `pnpm db:seed:dev` also creates 4 demo buyers and 12 orders at different stages (receipts to review, waiting for a transfer, ready, completed, new and completed cash orders, a return asked, and a refund to send after a shop declined), placed through the same RPCs the apps use. It runs only while the demo buyers have no orders.

### M3 installation (2026-10-14)
- **D-096 · Default** — **The slot engine lists; the database decides.** The engine is pure TypeScript (`supabase/functions/_shared/slots`), run by the `booking-slots` Edge Function on what `booking_context()` returns, for guests too. Holding a time is an RPC, `create_booking_hold()`, not an Edge Function as PLAN §5 had it: it checks again in SQL that the technician suits the job and is free (weekly hours, time off, other bookings), and the `EXCLUDE` constraint settles races (`SLOT_TAKEN`). *One place enforces the rules, and no service key is needed.* `booking-slots` and `notify-dispatch` are deployed without the platform's JWT check: the first passes the caller's role on to the database, the second checks its own token.
- **D-097 · Default** — **A fitting is always paid through an order.** Checkout takes the held booking (`p_booking_id`) and adds labour, travel and booking fees to the order. A fitting on its own (a part from an earlier order or bought elsewhere) is an order with no parts. The shops whose cart lines are in the booking send their whole sub-order to the technician (`TO_TECHNICIAN`). The hold lasts `slot_hold_minutes` until checkout; from checkout the booking waits in `AWAITING_PAYMENT` until the order's `pay_by`. *Amends D-023, where the hold ran until the receipt was sent: a buyer needs time to make the Bankak transfer.*
- **D-098 · Default** — **The parts follow the fitting.**
  - Once the technician accepts, the shop is told who collects the parts and when. The shop can hand them over only to an accepted booking, and the technician can set off or check in only with the parts in hand (PLAN §3.10 guard).
  - Cash orders: at hand-over the technician pays the shop for the parts (`CASH_HANDOVER`); the shop's hand-over is the record (D-011's "both confirm" is one tap for now). At the end of the job the technician collects all the cash from the buyer, recorded as `CASH_COLLECTED`.
  - The parts complete with the fitting. If they don't fit, a return opens on them for an admin to decide (D-019). If the fitting is cancelled, parts not yet fitted are cancelled, put back in stock and refunded. If the parts are cancelled or declined by the shop, the fitting is cancelled.
  - *Known gap:* declining a part-does-not-fit return on a cash order needs a manual ledger adjustment.
- **D-099 · Default** — **Cancellation charges.** Free until `cancel_cutoff_hours` before the start. Later, or once the technician has set off, the buyer pays the booking fee and the travel fee, up to what was paid upfront (the Bankak payment or the deposit): the booking fee goes to the platform first, the rest to the technician as travel compensation, and labour is refunded. A cash job with no deposit owes nothing. Cancelled by the technician (and nobody else free), by an admin or by expiry: everything is refunded. *With the default amounts this is exactly D-019's "the deposit is kept".*
- **D-100 · Default** — **Labour is one price per job type in the job**, added up over the job types involved (fitting three bulbs is one lighting job). It is locked when the time is held; if the job moves to another technician, they get the same price. The job's length is the sum of its items' minutes: the part's, else its category's, else 60.
- **D-101 · Default** — **The notification queue is a table, not pgmq** (amends D-007). Each alert row gets one `notification_deliveries` row per device of the right app (buyer, partner or admin, kept on the notification). A pg_cron job pokes `notify-dispatch` through pg_net once a minute when something is waiting; the address and token sit in Vault, set by `pnpm notify:setup <env>`. Failures retry after 1, 2, 4 and 8 minutes; dead tokens are dropped. SMS is recorded as `STUB` for key alerts, and push is a stub too until a Firebase service account is given (`--fcm`). Wording lives in `notification_templates` (Arabic and English, editable by admins); a test checks every code the database sends has both. *Retries, history and admin visibility come free with a table, and there's one less moving part.*
- **D-102 · Default** — **Who moves a stuck job.** A declined or unanswered offer goes to the next best technician free at the same time (ranked as PLAN §5); nobody free means `NEEDS_RESCHEDULE`. A technician who drops an accepted job is treated the same way, so `CANCELLED_BY_TECH` is never used. A job nobody accepted by 30 minutes before its start, or paid that late, needs a new time. `NEEDS_RESCHEDULE` left for 3 days expires with a full refund. Admins can pass a job on or cancel it from the console.
- **D-103 · Default** — **A wrong job code is an answer, not an error** (`{ok: false, error, attempts_left}`), so the attempt is counted. The fifth wrong code locks completion and tells the admins, who can complete the job from the console (D-028).
- **D-104 · Default** — **Technicians aren't readable as a table by buyers.** Buyers see `booking_context()` and `technician_profile()`, which leave out the home point and phone. The buyer gets the technician's phone once the job is accepted; the technician always sees the buyer's.
- **D-105 · Default** — **Reviews and disputes.** One review per received sub-order (the shop) and per finished fitting (the technician), changeable for 30 days; admins can hide one with a reason; triggers keep the averages that ranking uses. A dispute is between the buyer, the shop or technician involved, and the admins, with replies notifying the others; money is put right with the existing order and fitting tools.
- **D-106 · Default** — **The admin console has no sample data left.** Bookings (with a detail page), Disputes and technician applications read the database; Cash and Payouts include technicians; the overview counts technician applications, fittings needing attention and open disputes.
- **D-107 · Default** — **Demo installation data on dev:** 10 technicians (two still applying) and 5 fittings at the bay at different stages. Fittings at the buyer's place need the street's starting point, which the owner sets (D-077).
- **D-108 · Owner's request** — **A development brief** (`docs/BRIEF.md`, pointed to by `CLAUDE.md`) records the status and the rules every change follows, and is updated at the end of each milestone.
- **D-109 · Owner's setup** — **Push goes through one Firebase project, `alghali-street`, for every environment** (free Spark plan, FCM only). It has both Android apps (`sd.alghali.buyer`, `sd.alghali.partner`). The service account key lives in the ignored `secrets/` folder and is installed on dev and test with `pnpm notify:setup <env> --fcm …`; the live project gets it at launch. *Devices are per app, not per environment, so one project is enough; nothing else in Firebase is used.*

### M4 buyer app (2026-10-14)
- **D-110 · Default** — **The buyer app reads the real backend; the sample data stays for the partner app until M5.** The catalogue the screens read synchronously (cars, categories, zones, shops, the street's starting point) is the cached `catalogue_snapshot`, merged with each day's changes (`LiveCatalogue`, `mergeSnapshot`); parts, offers, technicians and reviews come from `search_parts`, `part_detail`, `booking-slots` and `reviews_for`. Lookups by id never throw: an unknown id gives an empty placeholder (a shop suspended after an order, a retired car), and orders carry the shop's name and phone as the order saw them. The demo controls, sample account and "demo" badges are gone from the buyer app.
- **D-111 · Default** — **One fitting per order, covering one or more cart parts** (the server holds one booking per buyer and pays one booking per order, D-097). "Add fitting" on a part opens the booking flow for that part plus any already in the fitting; changing it holds a new time (the server releases the old hold). Removing a fitted part drops the fitting. A fitting can also be booked for a part from an earlier order ("Book fitting" on a received part) or one bought elsewhere; those are paid through an order with no parts.
- **D-112 · Default** — **What works offline.** Reads are network first and fall back to the last saved answer per signed-in user (`CachedReads`, `DriftReadCache`): orders, fittings, garage, addresses, photo requests, inbox, chats, search results and part pages, plus the catalogue and settings, which are loaded before the first frame. Lists show the saved copy at once and refresh behind it; a banner says when the phone is offline. Changes that are safe to send later go through the outbox in order, each with its idempotency key and, for inserts, an id chosen on the phone so a replay can't duplicate: cart lines (mirrored to `cart_items`), garage cars, chat text, "mark read", ratings and profile changes. Everything that must be checked at once — checkout, holding a fitting time, receipts, photo requests, voice notes, cancelling, returns, complaints — needs a connection and says so. The outbox never sends a change ahead of an older one still waiting.
- **D-113 · Default** — **Push uses Firebase without the Google services Gradle plugin.** `pnpm env:sync` copies each app's Firebase entry from `secrets/google-services.json` into its `env/dev.json` (`FIREBASE_*`), and the app starts Firebase from those values. The buyer registers its token after signing in and when the token or language changes (`register_device` with the build number and language); alerts that arrive with the app open refresh what they're about and show a banner; tapping one opens its order, fitting, request, chat or complaint. Android gets an `alerts` channel (high importance) and a small white status-bar icon.
- **D-114 · Default** — **The inbox is worded on the phone from the admins' templates** (`notification_templates` for the buyer's codes, cached), with the same placeholder rules as the push dispatcher (`renderAlert`, twin of `_shared/notify/render.ts`), so the inbox and the push always say the same thing.
- **D-115 · Default** — **Bad connections:** reads retry once and give up after 20 seconds (`ReadPolicy`; set per query because the Supabase client applies its own options to function calls only), then fall back to saved data. Photos are shrunk on the phone before upload (receipts and request photos at most 1600 px, JPEG quality 70), listing thumbnails load first and full photos only when tapped. Voice notes are AAC at 32 kbit/s (about 240 KB a minute).
- **D-116 · Default** — **Addresses are GPS points.** "Use my current location" takes the phone's position, then the buyer adds a label and written directions (`addresses`). Delivery and travel fees shown before checkout are estimates from the straight-line distance to the shop's pin or the street's starting point; the server's are what count. While the street's starting point isn't set, fittings at the buyer's place and delivery show as unavailable.
- **D-117 · Default** — **The technician's live position is shown as distance and freshness, with "See on the map"** opening Google Maps, instead of an in-app map: no map SDK, no API key, and much less data.
- **D-118 · Default** — **Buyer app tests run on answers captured from alghali-dev** (`pnpm fixtures:buyer` → `apps/buyer/test/fixtures`), served by a fake Supabase (`FakeBackend`): every screen in Arabic and English at 360 dp, the fitting-to-checkout journey, a hold lost to another buyer, chat written offline and replayed once, and orders read offline. The happy path also runs on an emulator against dev (`pnpm buyer:e2e`), signing in as a demo buyer and cancelling the order it places.

### M5 partner app (2026-10-15)
- **D-119 · Default** — **"Closed today" is a time, not a status.** `shops.closed_until` (set by `shop_set_closed`, at most a week ahead; null opens again) makes `open_now` false in `shop_json` and `part_detail` without touching the weekly hours. The switch on the shop's home closes it until midnight in Khartoum. Orders can still come in; buyers just see the shop as closed. *A shop owner who has to leave for the day shouldn't edit seven days of hours and then remember to put them back.*
- **D-120 · Default** — **A shop's members read their own shop in any review state (`shop_detail`), and its sales (`shop_insights`).** A pending shop can already list stock (M1 allows writes while `PENDING`); the app shows a banner saying so, and rejected or suspended shops see the admin's reason. Insights count a sub-order on the Khartoum day it was placed, from when the shop can see it (paid, or a cash order placed) until it is cancelled, refused or returned; the five best sellers cover the same days.
- **D-121 · Default** — **The technician's job page says where the parts are.** `booking_json` lists each shop holding the fitting's parts with its status and items total (`parts`), so the job page shows "being prepared at …", "ready — collect it from …" or "collected", with a call button. On a cash fitting the technician pays each shop for its parts at pickup and collects labour, fees and parts from the buyer, less any deposit; the page shows both amounts. The app asks for at least one "after" photo before the buyer's code (the server doesn't). While a job is on the way the app sends the phone's position every 30 seconds, only while it is open (D-029).
- **D-122 · Default** — **What a partner can do offline.** Stock edits (one listing or a bulk price change) show at once and go out through the outbox as `shop_bulk_update`. Each listing's version rises by one per real change, so a second edit made before the first reaches the server carries the next version; an edit nobody changed is not sent, and one another phone beat is refused (`VERSION_CONFLICT`), reported, and the list reloaded. Quick listings are kept on the phone with their photo and sent when online: the photo goes up first under an id chosen on the phone (a replay overwrites the same files), then `shop_upsert_listing` with its idempotency key, then the photo is attached. A draft is a hidden listing. Chat text, read marks and profile changes are queued too. Order and job steps (accept, ready, hand over, on the way, check in, the buyer's code), offers on photo requests, hours and time off need a connection and say so.
- **D-123 · Default** — **One partner account, several homes.** An account can own or work at up to three shops and be a technician; the app opens one home at a time (picked automatically when there is only one, else on the role screen, switchable from the menu). The account is fetched before the app routes anywhere, so a partner never lands on the wrong screen while it loads. Money, staff and the shop profile show to owners only (staff manage orders and stock). Tapping an alert about the other home switches to it.
- **D-124 · Default** — **The technician's "accepting jobs" switch is time off.** Off = time off from now until midnight (reason `OFFLINE`); the quick block's 1 and 2 hours are `tech_quick_block` (`QUICK_BLOCK`); back on removes the breaks going on now. Planned days off are ordinary time off. *The slot engine already skips time off, so nothing new is needed on the server.*
- **D-125 · Default** — **Partners join in the app.** Sign up (name, email, phone, password), then apply as a shop (name, zone, plot, side, landmark, contacts, weekly hours, what it sells, makes) or as a technician (bay, home point from the phone's GPS, radius, a price per kind of job, makes, experience). An admin reviews it. Shop staff don't apply: they sign up and the owner adds them by email.
- **D-126 · Default** — **Listing photos are sized on the phone (D-008)** with `flutter_image_compress`: WebP `thumb` (160 px), `medium` (480 px) and `large` (1080 px) on the short side, plus the original JPEG, uploaded under one base key `shops/{shop}/listings/{id}/`.
- **D-127 · Default** — **Partner app tests run on answers captured from alghali-dev** as the demo shop owner and the demo technician (`pnpm fixtures:partner` → `apps/partner/test/fixtures`; anyone who isn't a demo account is replaced by a placeholder before saving), plus a new order, a photo request and a job offer the fake server adds. Every screen in Arabic and English at 360 dp; the order steps; stock edits and a quick listing made offline and replayed; an offer; closing for the day; a stock sheet; the job day with a wrong and a right code; the online switch; weekly hours; push; sign-in and applying. The happy path also runs on an emulator against dev (`pnpm partner:e2e`): the script places a cash order and books a fitting as a demo buyer, the shop accepts and readies the order, the technician accepts the job, and an admin cancels both afterwards.
- **D-128 · Default** — **Each app's inbox shows its own alerts** (`notifications.app`), so an account that buys and sells doesn't see shop alerts in the buyer app; the alert wording is cached for every code, since some reach both apps (`NEW_MESSAGE`, `BOOKING_CANCELLED`).
